Thesis

Sheet 01 / About

Most of my career has been spent selling things that are genuinely hard to sell — industrial equipment, energy infrastructure, technical solutions with buying committees stretching from the plant floor to the VP's office. That work taught me how technical products actually earn trust, one account at a time.

My MBA is teaching me how to turn that instinct into something more repeatable: prescriptive analytics for pricing and resource decisions, Tableau for turning account data into a story a room believes, and hands-on work with generative AI for building and positioning technical products.

This portfolio lives in the overlap — case studies that mix real commercial results with MBA projects where I did the positioning, market analysis, and storytelling work product marketing actually requires.

NOTE — This is a general-purpose portfolio, not tuned to one industry. The underlying skill set — positioning, account strategy, stakeholder analysis, and data-backed storytelling — is built to transfer across B2B technical, industrial, and SaaS environments alike.
